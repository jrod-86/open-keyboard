# Open Keyboard sticker store

`catalog.json` lists the sticker packs shown in Open Keyboard's sticker store. Every pack here must
be under a licence that allows redistribution (CC0, CC-BY, Apache-2.0, MIT...), with credit given in
the catalog.

| Pack | Artist | Licence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Yellow Moods (animated), Tuxedo Cat (animated) | jrod | CC0-1.0 |
| Cheeky Dog - Test (animated pilot) | jrod | CC0-1.0 |

To add a pack: add a zip of PNG/GIF/WebP pictures, a 128 px icon and an entry in `catalog.json`.

## Cheeky Dog - Test
One user-approved transparent animated WebP: good-morning coffee toast.
512 x 512, 72 frames, three seconds. Published as a separate pilot for phone testing
before expanding the pack. CC0 was explicitly authorized by jrod.
No GIF alternative is included in this initial WebP test; some receiving apps may
flatten animated WebP. No APK rebuild is required to load the updated catalogue.

## Yellow Moods (animated)
65 animated yellow emoji face stickers (animated WebP) made by jrod for Open Keyboard. CC0 (public domain).
GIF copies in `yellow-moods-alt/` are fetched one at a time for apps that flatten animated WebP (Messenger).

## Tuxedo Cat (animated)
64 animated tuxedo cat stickers (animated WebP) made by jrod for Open Keyboard. CC0 (public domain).
GIF copies in `tuxedo-cat-animated-alt/` are fetched one at a time for apps that flatten animated WebP (Messenger).
